### Decisions and risks

- Ordering is enforced as a strict chain
  (`event_time <= published_at <= collected_at <= available_at`) at the
  `TemporalRecord` level. **Risk:** real source data may occasionally
  report `published_at` before the nominal `event_time` is fully known
  (e.g. odds updates), so this constraint may need loosening once real
  timestamped odds data (P2-T001) is integrated — flagged here rather
  than silently relaxed now.
- `valid_from`/`valid_to` are treated as independent of the acquisition
  chain (a stat's validity window vs. when we learned about it); only
  internally ordered against each other.
- UTC is enforced strictly (`utcoffset() == 0`), not just "any aware
  datetime" — callers must call `to_utc()` before constructing a record.
