### Decisions and risks

- Tie-break for multiple valid+available versions of the same entity is
  "latest `available_at` wins" (most recently learned version), not
  latest `valid_from`. These usually agree but could diverge for
  backdated corrections — flagged as a modeling choice, not verified
  against a real backdated-correction case (none exists yet).
- `as_of` defaults to `prediction_at` (reconstruct "what do we know, as
  of now, about right now") but callers can pass a different `as_of` to
  reconstruct a past instant's state as known at a later prediction
  time — both are exercised by the golden fixtures.
